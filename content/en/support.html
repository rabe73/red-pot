Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Red Pot uses your iCloud. There is no account with the developer, because there is no developer-run service to have an account with.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Lists, checking off, the pantry and recipe suggestions all work offline — the last of these because the recipe components ship with the app. Without a connection only synchronisation with other devices pauses, and it catches up as soon as there is a network again.

Who needs a subscription?

Anyone who creates week plans or reads recipes needs their own — or belongs to the Family Sharing group of whoever subscribed. Without one, shopping lists, the pantry and the read-only week plan remain fully usable. This is not penny-pinching: a member's device cannot technically verify another person's subscription, and we would rather not make a claim that cannot be checked.

What happens if the person who created the household leaves?

A shared household lives in that person's iCloud. That is why export and import exist: another member can save the household and re-create it as their own.

Which languages?

German, English, French, Spanish and Italian.
